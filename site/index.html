The approach

Learn in layers, not shortcuts.

The repository organizes its material into progressive phases and companion references. Start with the foundations, build context, then choose specialist topics.

Before you begin

Learn responsibly.

Use cybersecurity knowledge only for lawful education, defensive work, and systems you own or have explicit permission to assess. Keep practical learning inside isolated labs and authorized training environments. Read the repository’s legal notice, security policy, and license terms before using or adapting its material.
